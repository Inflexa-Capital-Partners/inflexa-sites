## Key paragraphs (sample)

- An independent investment office that engineers payoff profiles — equity-like upside with credit-like downside — through bilateral, capacity-constrained managers and proprietary co-investment.
- Downside defined by seniority and a contractual minimum return; upside left open through warrants and conversion. The shape — not the direction of the market — is the product.
- Higher-for-longer rates, stuck private equity and crowded private credit make structured, downside-protected capital scarce. A 2027–2030 recap cycle, ramrodded by AI, is the backdrop.
- Issuers pay more to avoid dilution and added leverage — keeping control and most of the upside. Equity is diluted only in success.
- Founding-partner opportunity. Early-round / seed terms, first look at co-investment, and effective fees that decline — and can turn negative — as you introduce capital. A path to part-ownership of the platform.
- Next step — on signing an NDA we share the detailed strategy, terms, and a verifiable track record.
- A genuine third category of capital has emerged between private credit and private equity — equity-like upside with credit-like downside.
- Neither a control buyout nor a lender. Aligned capital that sits senior to common equity and can convert into it — typically a ~30% stake through a structured security.
- ≈90% of a private equity fund's upside with the downside profile of credit. The floor comes from seniority and a contractual minimum IRR; the upside from warrants and conversion.
- €1–3bn bespoke checks across 12–18 month negotiated deals. Few funds execute at scale, so the work earns a bespoke premium.
- Themes drawn from primary conversations with leading hybrid-capital managers, 2026.
- Strategic allocation is the primary long-run driver. Long horizons harvest mispricing and illiquidity; capital compounds when protected at the base.
- Valuation is the best signal of long-run return. Independent, in-depth fundamental analysis — we underwrite issuers, not macro forecasts.
- A few positions understood deeply. High conviction over wide diversification — excess return without spreading thin.
- Risk is impairment of capital, not volatility. Volatility creates opportunity; the downside is defined before a position exists.
- Founder capital alongside clients on identical terms. Owners and creditors — not traders of paper. A small, senior, accountable team.
- A Luxembourg RAIF as a SICAV-SCSp — tax-transparent, ring-fenced compartments, and a close analogue to the LP structure institutions already understand.
- Compartment 1 is the commitment fund; Compartment 2 is the no-fee co-investment sidecar. Future verticals open on the same chassis.
- Hosted AIFM, Luxembourg depositary and administrator; an ISIN feeder bridges private-bank clients. Advisory now, discretionary on licensing.
- Underlying funds run ~8–10 years; the programme is termed to ~12–15 years with an over-commitment strategy and a capital-call line — never a forced secondary sale.
- A collaboration for early-round and seed investors — up to €250m of founding-partner capital.
- Past a critical scale, the economics you earn on introduced capital fully offset — then exceed — the fees on your own capital. You become an aligned distribution partner, not a passive LP.
- Next step — on signing an NDA we share the detailed strategy, terms, and a verifiable track record (independently confirmable).
