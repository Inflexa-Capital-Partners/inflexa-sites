02 — Why now

Why now is the moment for solutions

Rates stay higher for longer

  • Expensive debt makes added leverage costly.
  • Issuers need capital that doesn't enlarge the leverage stack or hit ratings.

Private equity is stuck

  • Legacy portfolios are over-levered and short of DPI.
  • Sponsors take preferred equity to delever, fund growth and return capital — without ceding control.

Private credit is crowded

  • Direct-lending returns compressed and docs loosened.
  • Many opportunistic peers became direct lenders — fewer left to structure the hard deals.

A CFO win-win

  • Companies pay more to avoid dilution and added leverage — keeping control and most of the upside.
  • Equity is diluted only in success: total alignment.
A 2027–2030 recapitalisation and distress cycle — ramrodded by AI — is the shared backdrop. The structuring premium is highest when capital is scarce.

04 — Our approach

Our approach to investing

A disciplined framework

A repeatable process — not luck or access. An unbiased method that limits behavioural bias and doesn't depend on any one person.

Allocation drives returns

Strategic allocation is the primary long-run driver. Long horizons harvest mispricing and illiquidity; capital compounds when protected at the base.

Bottom-up underwriting

Valuation is the best signal of long-run return. Independent, in-depth fundamental analysis — we underwrite issuers, not macro forecasts.

Concentrated conviction

A few positions understood deeply. High conviction over wide diversification — excess return without spreading thin.

Risk is permanent loss

Risk is impairment of capital, not volatility. Volatility creates opportunity; the downside is defined before a position exists.

Aligned ownership

Founder capital alongside clients on identical terms. Owners and creditors — not traders of paper. A small, senior, accountable team.

05 — The structure

How we structure the fund & co-investment sidecar

One umbrella

A Luxembourg RAIF as a SICAV-SCSp — tax-transparent, ring-fenced compartments, and a close analogue to the LP structure institutions already understand.

Two compartments

Compartment 1 is the commitment fund; Compartment 2 is the no-fee co-investment sidecar. Future verticals open on the same chassis.

Institutional spine

Hosted AIFM, Luxembourg depositary and administrator; an ISIN feeder bridges private-bank clients. Advisory now, discretionary on licensing.

Underlying funds run ~8–10 years; the programme is termed to ~12–15 years with an over-commitment strategy and a capital-call line — never a forced secondary sale.
